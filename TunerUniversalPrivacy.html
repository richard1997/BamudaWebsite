TunerUniversal Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 25 November, 2025

Information We Collect

  • No personal data is collected — you don’t need an account to use TunerUniversal.
  • Microphone access is used only for tuning. Audio is processed locally on your device and never stored or shared.
  • Preferences (such as tuning settings or UI options) are saved only on your device.
  • No personally identifiable analytics are collected. If anonymous diagnostics are ever used, they are aggregated and cannot be used to identify you.

In‑App Purchases

TunerUniversal may offer optional in‑app purchases to unlock additional features or enhancements. All purchases are processed securely by Apple. We do not have access to your payment information.

How We Use Information

We use information solely to provide accurate tuning functionality, improve app performance, and troubleshoot technical issues. We do not sell, store, or share your personal information.

Data Storage & Security

All audio processing happens locally on your device. Any preferences or settings are stored only on your device. We implement reasonable safeguards to protect your information and maintain the security of the app.

Children’s Privacy

TunerUniversal is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted within the app or on our official website.
