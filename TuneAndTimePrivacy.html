TuneAndTime Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 25 November, 2025

Information We Collect

  • No personal data is collected — you don’t need an account to use TuneAndTime.
  • Microphone access is used only for tuning. Audio is processed locally and never stored or shared.
  • Preferences (like metronome settings) are saved only on your device.
  • Optional anonymous analytics may be used to improve performance, but never linked to you.
  • Advertising SDKs used in TuneAndTime — including Google AdMob, Vungle, Unity Ads, InMobi, Chartboost, and their mediation partners — may collect and share device identifiers (such as Advertising ID, Android ID, IP address, and other device signals) for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. These identifiers are processed by the respective advertising partners according to their own privacy policies and are not stored by us.

How We Use Information

We use information solely to provide accurate tuner and metronome functionality, improve app performance, and troubleshoot technical issues. Advertising partners may use device identifiers to deliver ads, measure ad performance, personalize ad content, and prevent invalid activity. We do not sell or store your personal information.

Data Storage & Security

All processing happens locally on your device. If analytics are enabled, data is anonymized and aggregated. Advertising identifiers are processed by Google AdMob, Vungle, Unity Ads, InMobi, Chartboost, and their mediation partners according to their own privacy policies. We implement reasonable safeguards to protect your information.

Children’s Privacy

TuneAndTime is safe for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted within the app or on our official site.
