TuneAndTime Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 25 November, 2025

Information We Collect

  • No personal data is collected — you don’t need an account to use TuneAndTime.
  • Microphone access is used only for tuning. Audio is processed locally on your device and never stored or shared.
  • Preferences (such as metronome settings) are saved only on your device.
  • Optional anonymous analytics may be used to improve performance. These analytics are aggregated and never linked to you.
  • Advertising in TuneAndTime is provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as Advertising ID, IP address, and device signals) for ad delivery, measurement, personalization, and fraud prevention. These identifiers are processed by Google according to their own privacy policies and are not stored by us.
  • TuneAndTime uses Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework on supported devices. If you grant permission, your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA) may be accessed by AdMob for the purpose of delivering personalized ads. If you decline, ads will still appear but will not be personalized.
  • TuneAndTime also uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent for personalized or non‑personalized ads where required by law. Your ad preferences are managed through this consent flow.

In‑App Purchases

TuneAndTime offers optional in‑app purchases, including subscriptions that remove ads or unlock additional features. All purchases are processed securely by Apple. We do not have access to your payment information.

How We Use Information

We use information solely to provide accurate tuner and metronome functionality, improve app performance, and troubleshoot technical issues. Google AdMob may use device identifiers to deliver ads, measure ad performance, personalize ad content (based on your consent), and prevent invalid activity. We do not sell, store, or share your personal information.

Data Storage & Security

All audio processing happens locally on your device. If analytics are enabled, data is anonymized and aggregated. Advertising identifiers are processed by Google AdMob according to their privacy policies. We implement reasonable safeguards to protect your information.

Children’s Privacy

TuneAndTime is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted within the app or on our official website.
