Intervals • Pro Trainer

Intervals • Pro Trainer is a musician‑first ear‑training tool designed to help you recognise intervals quickly, confidently, and musically. Whether you're learning by ear, improvising, transcribing, or strengthening your musical intuition, Intervals • Pro Trainer gives you a clean, focused environment to master interval recognition.

Features

  • Melodic interval playback (ascending & descending)
  • Clean, minimalist interface designed for musicians
  • Free intervals: Major 2nd, Major 3rd, Octave
  • Pro intervals: Minor 2nd → Major 7th, Perfect 4th, Tritone, Perfect 5th, 6ths, 7ths
  • Random Interval Mode for advanced training
  • Selectable root notes (MIDI 48–72)
  • Warm, musical ToneSynth playback engine
  • No ads, no tracking, no data collection

Why Musicians Love It

Intervals • Pro Trainer focuses on clarity and musicality. Every sound is generated locally using a warm, expressive synthesizer designed for ear training. No distractions, no clutter — just clean interval practice that builds real musicianship.

In‑App Purchases

Unlock the full interval library and Random Interval Mode with a one‑time purchase. All transactions are processed securely by Apple.

Privacy

Intervals • Pro Trainer collects no personal data. All processing happens locally on your device. For full details, see the Privacy Policy.
