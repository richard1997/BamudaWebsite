Chord • Pro

A musician‑first harmony engine for building expressive chords, exploring voicings, designing progressions, and hearing your ideas come alive — instantly and beautifully.

Build Chords With Musical Intelligence

Chord • Pro lets you construct chords from the ground up — Root, Category, Quality, Extensions, Inversions, and advanced color tones. Every choice updates the voicing in real time, giving you instant musical feedback.

  • All voicings generated by a context‑aware, musician‑inspired engine.
  • Supports Drop‑2, Drop‑3, Spread, Bass Emphasis, and Humanization.
  • Jazz, pop, gospel, and R&B voicing styles built in.
  • Hear every chord with rich, expressive playback.

Design Progressions That Feel Alive

Create chord progressions with intuitive tools that stay out of your way. Save, load, reorder, and experiment freely — every progression is stored locally on your device.

  • Block, arpeggio up, and arpeggio down playback styles.
  • Instant MIDI export for DAWs and notation software.
  • Local JSON‑based saving for complete privacy.

Learn Through Sound, Not Menus

Chord • Pro is built for musicians who learn by listening and experimenting. Every feature is designed to teach through interaction — not text walls.

  • Beginner mode introduces chord basics with clarity.
  • Intermediate and Advanced modes unlock full voicing control.
  • Context‑aware scale suggestions help you understand harmonic function.

Privacy‑First Design

No accounts. No tracking. No analytics. All processing happens on your device. Your chords, progressions, and preferences stay with you — always.
