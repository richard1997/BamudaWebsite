Ear Training Pro

Ear Training Pro is a musician‑first training tool designed to help you build confident, reliable listening skills. With clean visuals, responsive audio, and a distraction‑free interface, it supports players of all levels — from beginners learning intervals to advanced musicians refining chord quality recognition, scale degrees, and rhythmic accuracy.

Core Features

  • Interval Recognition — ascending, descending, harmonic
  • Chord Quality Identification — triads, sevenths, extended chords
  • Scale Degree Training — functional ear training in any key
  • Rhythm Training — subdivisions, patterns, and timing accuracy
  • Custom Practice Mode — choose your own sets, ranges, and difficulty
  • Progress Tracking — accuracy, streaks, and level progression
  • Clean, musician‑first UI with no clutter or distractions

Designed for Musicians

Every exercise is generated locally on your device using deterministic, testable logic. Audio playback is immediate and responsive, ensuring a smooth training experience. Whether you are preparing for exams, improving improvisation, or strengthening your musical intuition, Ear Training Pro adapts to your pace.

Premium Experience

Advanced modes, extended difficulty levels, and additional training packs are available as optional in‑app purchases. All purchases are processed securely by Apple.

Privacy‑First

Ear Training Pro collects no personal data. All processing, progress tracking, and preferences remain on your device. For full details, see the Ear Training Pro Privacy Policy.

Availability

Ear Training Pro is available on the Apple App Store.
