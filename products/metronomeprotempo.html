Metronome • Pro Tempo

Metronome • Pro Tempo is a professional‑grade rhythm tool designed for musicians who demand precision, clarity, and flexibility. Built with a modern rhythm engine and a clean interface, it delivers stable tempo, advanced subdivisions, polyrhythms, and guided tempo training.

Features

  • Rock‑solid metronome engine with ultra‑stable timing.
  • Advanced Subdivision Mode for triplets, swing, and custom groupings.
  • Polyrhythm Mode for 2:3, 3:4, 5:4, and custom ratios.
  • Trainer Mode with gradual tempo increase/decrease.
  • Beautiful, minimal interface designed for clarity and focus.
  • All rhythm processing happens locally on your device.
  • No ads, no tracking, no data collection.

Designed for Musicians

Metronome • Pro Tempo is crafted with a musician‑first philosophy. Every interaction — from tapping tempo to adjusting subdivisions — is designed to feel intuitive and expressive. Whether you're practicing scales, preparing for performance, or exploring complex rhythms, the app adapts to your workflow.

Privacy & Trust

Metronome • Pro Tempo collects no personal data. For full details, see the Metronome • Pro Tempo Privacy Policy.

Availability

Metronome • Pro Tempo is available now on the Apple App Store for iPhone and iPad.
