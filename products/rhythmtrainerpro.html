Rhythm Trainer Pro

A musician‑first rhythm training experience.

Overview

Rhythm Trainer Pro is a premium rhythm development tool designed for musicians of all levels. Featuring Tap Match, Subdivision Identification, and Polyrhythm Recognition, the app helps you build timing accuracy, groove awareness, and rhythmic confidence.

Key Features

  • Tap Match mode for real‑time timing accuracy training.
  • Subdivision ID mode for recognizing rhythmic groupings.
  • Polyrhythm ID mode with dual‑lane visualizer and lane pulses.
  • Advanced rhythm engine with groove feel, micro‑timing, and style selection.
  • Visualizer with beat tracking, hit markers, and pulse feedback.
  • Detailed scoring and accuracy graph after each session.
  • Countdown overlay for clean session starts.

Pro Features

  • Unlock Tuplets and Polyrhythm training.
  • Access advanced groove and micro‑timing controls.
  • Future updates including groove templates and custom polyrhythms.

Designed for Musicians

Every detail of Rhythm Trainer Pro is crafted with a musician‑first philosophy. From the responsive visualizer to the precise audio engine, the app provides a clean, intuitive, and expressive rhythm training environment.

Availability

Rhythm Trainer Pro is available on both the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.
