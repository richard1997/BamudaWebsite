MetricSmith – Fast, Accurate, and Customisable

MetricSmith is a powerful, beautifully designed unit converter built for engineers, students, scientists, and everyday users. Convert values instantly, customise your workflow, and stay productive with a clean, distraction‑free interface.

Current Features

  • ⚡Instant Conversions – Hundreds of units across dozens of categories.
  • 🧩Custom Units – Create your own units for specialised tasks.
  • 📁Custom Categories – Build personalised conversion groups.
  • 📊Batch Mode – Convert multiple values at once.
  • 🎨Themes – Beautiful colour themes and layout options.
  • 🔒Privacy‑Focused – All processing happens locally on your device.
  • 🚫No Ads – Clean, uninterrupted workflow.

Designed for Clarity

MetricSmith is crafted for precision and ease of use. Whether you're converting engineering values, scientific measurements, or everyday units, the interface stays clean and distraction‑free. Every detail is designed to help you work quickly and confidently.

Privacy & Trust

MetricSmith collects no personal data. All conversions, custom units, and preferences are processed entirely on your device and never transmitted. For full details, see the MetricSmith Privacy Policy.

Availability

MetricSmith are available on both the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

Why Choose MetricSmith?

  • Simple, intuitive design for fast, accurate conversions.
  • Customisable tools for engineering, science, and everyday use.
  • Offline, private, and built with long‑term reliability in mind.
