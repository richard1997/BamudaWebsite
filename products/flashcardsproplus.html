FlashCards Pro+ – Learn Faster, Remember Longer

FlashCards Pro+ is a focused, distraction‑free study companion designed to help you master anything: music theory, languages, exams, or personal knowledge. With a clean interface and powerful spaced repetition engine, it keeps you in flow while making every review session count.

Current Features

  • 🃏Flexible Decks – Create unlimited decks and cards with text, emojis, and rich formatting.
  • ⏰Spaced Repetition – Smart scheduling to review cards just before you forget them.
  • 📊Progress Tracking – See your streaks, accuracy, and long‑term retention at a glance.
  • 🎯Focused Study Modes – Study by deck, tag, or difficulty to target what matters most.
  • 🌙Light & Dark Themes – Comfortable visuals for long sessions, day or night.
  • 📱Offline First – All decks and progress are stored locally on your device.
  • ⚙️Customizable Sessions – Control session length, card order, and answer reveal behavior.
  • ⭐Favorites & Marking – Flag tricky cards to revisit more often.

Why Choose FlashCards Pro+

  • Designed for deep, focused study without clutter or distractions.
  • Built around proven spaced repetition principles to maximize retention.
  • Ideal for musicians, students, professionals, and lifelong learners.
  • No accounts, no ads, and no tracking—just pure learning.

Availability

Flashcards Pro+ are available on both the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.
