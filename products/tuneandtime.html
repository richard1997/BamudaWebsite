TuneAndTime – Your Essential Music Companion

TuneAndTime helps musicians stay perfectly in tune, on time, and musically confident. Whether practicing scales, learning harmony, or training your ear, it provides reliable tools to sharpen your skills and deepen your understanding.

Current Features

  • 🎯Precision Tuner – Fast, accurate pitch detection for all instruments.
  • ⏱️Metronome – Adjustable tempo, time signatures, and sound options.
  • 🎼All Scales Trainer – Major, minor, and advanced scale patterns with playback.
  • 🔢Degree Trainer – Learn scale degrees and strengthen tonal awareness.
  • 🌈Modes Explorer – Ionian to Locrian with note patterns and audio examples.
  • 🎹Chord Construction – Build chords, view intervals, and hear voicings instantly.
  • 🌀Circle of Fifths – Understand key relationships and harmonic movement.
  • 👂Ear Trainer – Interval, chord, and melodic recognition exercises.
