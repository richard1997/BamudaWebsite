Bamuda Support

Welcome to the official support page for all Bamuda mobile applications. If you need help with any of our apps, you’ll find contact details, general troubleshooting steps, and frequently asked questions below.

General Troubleshooting

  • Restart the app and your device
  • Ensure you are signed in with the correct Apple ID or Google account
  • Check for available app updates
  • Verify that your device has internet access (for purchases or downloads)
  • Reinstall the app if issues persist (ensure you have backed up your data if applicable)

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the app → Go to Settings → Tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Do your apps require an account?

No. All Bamuda apps are fully private and work entirely offline.

Where is my data stored?

All data is stored locally on your device. None of our apps collect or transmit personal information.

How do I back up my data?

Some Bamuda apps include a built‑in backup or export feature. If available, you can find it in the app’s Settings section.
