FlashCards Pro+ Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 15 April, 2026

Information We Collect

  • FlashCards Pro+ does not require an account or login. We do not collect your name, email, or contact details.
  • All flashcard content, decks, study progress, and scheduling logic are stored locally on your device.
  • Your preferences (such as themes, study modes, reminders, or layout options) are stored only on your device.
  • We do not use third‑party analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking identifiers, or profiling tools.

In‑App Purchases

FlashCards Pro+ offers optional one‑time purchases and/or subscriptions to unlock additional features, advanced study modes, or premium content. All transactions are processed securely by Apple via the App Store. We do not have access to your full payment information, such as credit card numbers or billing details.

How We Use Information

Any information stored on your device is used solely to provide and improve the flashcard learning experience, including spaced repetition, progress tracking, and personalization of study sessions. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

Data Storage & Security

All study data, decks, and preferences are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to our servers. If you choose to use system‑level backup services (such as iCloud backup), your data may be included in those backups according to Apple’s policies. We rely on the security features of iOS and the App Store to help protect your data.

Children’s Privacy

FlashCards Pro+ is suitable for learners of all ages and can be used by children under adult supervision. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can address the situation.

Third‑Party Services

FlashCards Pro+ does not integrate third‑party analytics, advertising networks, or social media SDKs. Any links to external websites (such as support or documentation) are opened outside the app and are governed by the privacy policies of those respective sites.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements to FlashCards Pro+, changes in legal requirements, or updates to our practices. When we make changes, we will update the “Effective Date” at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
